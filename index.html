Cybersecurity Profile & Background

The Dual Advantage: Attacker Mindset Meets Builder Engineering

I am Mohamed ElAzab, a Cybersecurity student at the Faculty of Computers and Information, Zagazig University (Expected Graduation: 2028). My focus is anchored in Offensive Security and Network Security, specifically Web Application Penetration Testing, protocol analysis, vulnerability research, and Layer-2 infrastructure hardening.

My methodology is rooted in authentic laboratory validation: analyzing attack surfaces in isolated targets, reproducing documented CVEs, verifying theoretical vulnerabilities with reproducible Proof-of-Concept (PoC) evidence, and designing defensible hardening guidelines.

Complementing my security specialty is an extensive background in Full-Stack .NET Development and Relational Database Engineering through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI). Having built enterprise C# APIs and normalized SQL schemas, I evaluate codebases with an insider's clarity on where validation fails, how queries get concatenated, and why misconfigurations occur.
